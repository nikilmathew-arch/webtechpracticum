About the hospital

Riverside General Hospital is a 450-bed multi-specialty hospital that has served the community since 1994. Our teams provide emergency care, planned surgery, maternity and child health, and long-term treatment under one roof.

We are accredited for quality and patient safety, and every patient receives a single care plan that is shared across all the doctors involved.

Book an appointment or browse our departments.

Departments

Our clinical departments and where to find them:

Cardiology

Diagnosis and treatment of heart and blood vessel conditions.

  • ECG, echo and stress tests
  • Angiography and angioplasty
  • Cardiac rehabilitation

Orthopaedics

Care for bones, joints and sports injuries.

  • Joint replacement
  • Fracture care
  • Physiotherapy

Paediatrics

Health care for infants, children and teenagers.

  • Vaccination clinic
  • Newborn care unit
  • Child development checks

Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Pregnancy, childbirth and women's health.

  • Antenatal clinic
  • Labour and delivery suites
  • Fertility counselling

Neurology

Disorders of the brain, spine and nerves.

  • EEG and nerve studies
  • Stroke unit
  • Headache clinic

General Medicine

First point of care for adults with common or complex illness.

  • Diabetes and blood pressure care
  • Preventive health checks
  • Infectious disease clinic

Emergency information

24-hour Emergency Department

Trauma, stroke, heart attack, burns and obstetric emergencies are treated on arrival, without waiting for an appointment.

Emergency hotline: +91 422 200 0108
Ambulance: 108

Go to the emergency department if you have:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Sudden weakness, slurred speech or facial droop
  • Severe bleeding or a serious injury
  • Difficulty breathing
